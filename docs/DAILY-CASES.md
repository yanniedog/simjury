# Daily case authoring — retired

The `dd-*` schema, seven-case library and daily authoring system were retired by
owner decision on 2026-08-03. Their source and media are preserved under
`archive/daily-v2-2026-08-03/` and are not shipped.

New work must use the single Court Week model and legal-order invariants in
[`../COURT-WEEK.md`](../COURT-WEEK.md). The continuing safety requirements are:

- fiction is schema-pinned and disclosed once at the adult entry;
- no real people, organisations, brands, events or specific real places;
- serious but non-graphic treatment and no reusable instructions for harm;
- reviewed, pre-authored player-facing words only; and
- spoiler separation before open-court verdict return.
