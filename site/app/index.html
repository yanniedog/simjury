SimJury: The Daily Docket

Take Juror 01's seat in one serious criminal case each day. The interactive sitting takes about twenty minutes and requires JavaScript; this summary keeps the complete public journey readable when interaction is unavailable.

  1. Choose today's featured sitting or any case in the seven-case library, including the guided introduction.
  2. Read the case briefing and its case-specific content advisory.
  3. Hear both advocates' opening statements.
  4. Examine each item of evidence in order and keep private notes.
  5. Hear both closing statements and enter the jury room.
  6. Test your reasoning with eleven authored jurors; optionally open or join a private live-human beta room where invited people deliberate alongside them.
  7. Record Guilty, Not Guilty, or Undecided / No verdict after deliberation. Any juror may remain undecided, and the jury can hang.
  8. Hear the judge's unanimous, majority, or hung-jury result, examine the reveal, and create a spoiler-safe share card.

Narration is optional. Progress, notes, verdicts, preferences, and sitting statistics are saved in this browser only; there is no account or cross-device sync. If you opt into a live jury, its messages and stated positions use short-lived room state.
