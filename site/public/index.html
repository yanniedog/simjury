Inside the sitting

A short path from evidence to judgment.

  1. 01
    Hear both sides

    Meet the accused, hear both openings and examine each piece of evidence in order.

  2. 02
    Deliberate from the evidence

    Choose the evidence that matters and test your reasoning with eleven jurors. This is single-player: there are no live players or chat.

  3. 03
    Lock your verdict

    Decide Guilty or Not Guilty only after deliberation. Your verdict is final for that sitting.

Modern judgment

The evidence is shared. The reasoning is yours.

Memory, motive, fairness and doubt shape what every juror notices. SimJury makes that private process visible—without scores, live opponents or a feed demanding your attention.
