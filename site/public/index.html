Inside Court Week

A real trial rhythm, not a stack of summaries.

  1. 01
    Serve through the court week

    Hear the plea, openings, witnesses, objections, exhibits, closings and the judge's final directions in their lawful order.

  2. 02
    Deliberate from admitted evidence

    Seal your provisional view, see only the anonymous aggregate ballot, and reason with eleven authored jurors through the weekend.

  3. 03
    Return to open court

    The jury may return Murder, manslaughter, Not Guilty, or remain unable to agree. No outcome is labelled the correct answer.

Modern judgment

The evidence is shared. The reasoning is yours.

Memory, motive, fairness and doubt shape what every juror notices. SimJury makes that private process visible—without scores, leaderboards or a feed demanding your attention.
