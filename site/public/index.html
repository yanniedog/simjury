Inside the sitting

A short path from evidence to judgment.

  1. 01
    Hear both sides

    Meet the accused, hear both openings and examine each piece of evidence in order.

  2. 02
    Deliberate from the evidence

    Choose the evidence that matters and raise your own concerns. Eleven authored jurors respond; when the optional live-jury beta is available, invited people can deliberate in the same room alongside them.

  3. 03
    Reach your view; hear the room

    Record Guilty, Not Guilty, or Undecided / No verdict after deliberation. Any juror may remain undecided, so the room can return a unanimous verdict, majority verdict, or a hung jury with no verdict.

Keep in touch
